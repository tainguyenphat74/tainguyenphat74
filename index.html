Little Harvest needs WebGL

This game requires JavaScript and WebGL graphics. If the farm does not load, enable them in your browser, then retry.

How to play

Harvest 6 crops and earn 60 coins. Select Bed 1–6 or tap a soil bed to walk beside it. Plant seeds, water, wait 12 seconds, then harvest using the action button or F. Each harvest earns 10 coins; seeds and water are free.

Focus the scene to walk with WASD or arrow keys. Click or tap open ground to move. Drag to orbit; scroll, pinch, or use + / − to zoom. ⌂ resets the camera.

New season immediately resets all crops and coins. Progress saves in this browser only; watered crops grow while you are away. You can keep farming after reaching the goal.

Press Escape or Close help to return. Movement stops while help is open.
